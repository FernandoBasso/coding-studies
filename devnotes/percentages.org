* Intro

When we say something like “16 is 20% of 80”, 16 is the /percentage/, 20
is the /percent/, and 80 is the base (or quantity).

Let \(p\) be the /percent/, \(n\) be the /percentage/, and \(b\) be the /base/.

* Find the percentage of some base

For example, what is 20% of 80?

Formula:

\begin{equation}
\frac{p}{100} * b = n
\end{equation}

Substituting and solving:

- \(\frac{20}{100} \times{80} = n\)
- \(0.2 \times{80} = n\)
- \(16 = n\)
