* Intro

When we say something like “16 is 20% of 80”:

- 16 is the /percentage/ or the *part* \(p\).
- 20 is the /percent/ or *ratio* \(r\).
- 80 is the /quantity/ or *base* \(b\).


* Find the percentage of some base

For example, what is 20% of 80?

Formula:

\begin{equation}
\frac{r}{100} * b = p
\end{equation}

Substituting and solving:

- \(\frac{20}{100} \times{80} = p\)
- \(0.2 \times{80} = p\)
- \(16 = p\)
